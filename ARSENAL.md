# Organ Ledger — consolidated arsenal

Updated 2026-10-14. One file for the notes that are still worth keeping.

Research record only. Not medical advice. Not a dosing guide. Nothing here is a prescription.

The individual files in [notes/](notes/) stay as the source write-ups. This page is the one to read. Items marked closed were checked and are not leads.

## How a note earns a place

It names a tissue, a mechanism, and a limit. A tweet that recycles an old paper, invents a device, or skips the species does not.

Partial reprogramming is the through-line. Gill 2022 showed a clock can move in a dish if the factors stop before the cell becomes a stem cell. ER-100 is the first attempt to do a safer, switchable version of that idea inside one human organ. Everything else is a local repair tool or a detection tool.

## Skin — the clock moved in a dish

**MPTR fibroblasts.** Gill et al., *eLife* 2022. DOI [10.7554/eLife.71624](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.71624).

Yamanaka factors were turned on in dermal fibroblasts from middle-aged donors only until the maturation phase, then withdrawn. The cells briefly lost fibroblast identity and got it back. A transcriptome clock and the DNA methylation clock both shifted about 30 years younger. Collagen output and migration speed partly recovered.

Not a treatment. The cells were engineered in a dish, the pulse included c-Myc, and no person’s skin changed age. An October 2026 post recycled this 2022 paper as breaking news. The treatment path is a short, local, switchable pulse without Myc, or a normal drug that copies the window. Neither is this experiment.

## Eye — the only partial-reprogramming program in a person

**ER-100.** Life Biosciences. OSK factors, no MYC, AAV into one eye, chemical on/off. Glaucoma and NAION. Phase 1 safety study. First patient dosed June 2026.

One eye, not the body. Safety first. This is the in-person descendant of the Gill window, not a skin cream and not a lifespan drug.

## Brain

**Hevin / SPARCL-1.** Cabral-Miranda et al., *Aging Cell* 2025. DOI [10.1111/acel.14493](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.14493). PMID 39935382.

AAV overexpression of Hevin in hippocampal astrocytes improved object memory in middle-aged Alzheimer-model mice and in middle-aged wild-type mice. Synapses changed. Plaque load did not fall. Mouse gene therapy. The tweet that said it clears waste overstated the paper.

**LRP1 polymersomes.** *Signal Transduction and Targeted Therapy* 2025;10:331. DOI [10.1038/s41392-025-02426-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41392-025-02426-1).

Particles aimed at LRP1 on the blood-brain barrier. In Alzheimer-model mice, brain amyloid fell about 45% and plasma amyloid rose about 8-fold within 2 hours. The tweet’s “50–60% within one hour” is higher and faster than the paper. Mice only. Approved antibodies already exist and carry ARIA risk. This route has no human safety data.

**Stalled neurogenesis in depression.** Peng et al., *Nature Medicine*, 21 Aug 2026. DOI [10.1038/s41591-026-04571-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04571-8).

Adult human hippocampus still has a neurogenic lineage. In nonmedicated major depression that process looks stalled. Postmortem map. No drug named.

**Omega-3 (DHA / EPA).** Crupi et al., *Current Medicinal Chemistry* 2013. PMID [23746276](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23746276/).

A review, not a new trial. It summarizes animal and earlier work in which n-3 fats are tied to adult hippocampal neurogenesis, long-term potentiation, and new dendritic spines. A capsule is not a proven neuron factory in people.

**Optogenetics.** 2026 Nobel in Physiology or Medicine: Deisseroth, Hegemann, Nagel. A research switch for defined neurons. Human use so far is mostly the eye, not a brain eraser. Does not by itself extend lifespan.

## Heart

**Urolithin A in stiff-heart failure.** Su et al., *Science Advances* 2026;12(34). DOI [10.1126/sciadv.aec8088](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aec8088).

In a mouse model of heart failure with preserved ejection fraction, one week of urolithin A improved relaxation (the King’s group said up to 80% on the measure they tracked) by acting at PKGIα Cys42. Lab-grown human heart tissue relaxed faster. Not a human heart trial. Gut production varies, so food is not a dose. Supplements sold for muscle are a different claim.

**A transplanted heart takes the host’s age.** Poganik et al., bioRxiv, 17 Sep 2026. DOI [10.64898/2026.09.15.751836](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.15.751836).

Old mouse grafts looked younger in young hosts. Young grafts aged faster in old hosts. The host’s own clock did not move the other way. A small human set pointed the same direction. Preprint. Not evidence that young blood rewinds your own heart. The neighborhood can overwrite a local edit.

## Lungs

**CMR316.** Inhaled DPP4 inhibitor aimed at alveolar type 2 stem cells. Phase 1/1b completed ([NCT06589219](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06589219)). Safety only. No efficacy readout.

**LTI-03.** Inhaled caveolin-1 peptide in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. Phase 2 (RENEW, Rein Therapeutics). Fast Track is not approval.

**Buloxibutid / C21.** Oral AT2 receptor agonist. Phase 2b enrolled (Vicore ASPIRE). Topline aimed mid-2027.

**SMS cells.** Nebulized allogeneic blood-derived stem cells for COPD (SMSbiotech). Early human studies. Not a cure.

**REGEND001.** A patient’s own bronchial basal cells returned by bronchoscope (Regend Therapeutics). The furthest version of this idea is in China. The U.S. path is early.

## Legs and vessels

**AMG0001 / Collategene.** Intramuscular HGF plasmid to grow small vessels. Faster ulcer healing in a limb-ischemia phase 2 (LEGenD-1). Not U.S.-approved.

**Humacyte ATEV (Symvess).** Lab-grown human vessel. Approved for some trauma repairs. Peripheral artery disease and critical-limb use are still in trials.

## Joints and bone

**15-PGDH inhibitor.** Singla, Blau, Bhutani et al., *Science* 2025. DOI [10.1126/science.adx6649](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adx6649). PMID 41308124. Tool compound SW033291.

15-PGDH is about twice as high in aged mouse knee cartilage as in young cartilage. Inhibiting it regenerated hyaline cartilage, not scar cartilage, in aged mice, in mice after an ACL-type injury, and in human osteoarthritic knee explants. Existing chondrocytes changed job. Stem cells were not the driver. No human joint trial. A related oral inhibitor has been studied in muscle, not knees.

**Glycocalyx-edited marrow cells.** Moraleda et al., *Cell* 2026. [NCT02566655](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02566655).

A person’s own marrow cells given a bone-homing sugar tag, one IV dose. Fracture rate fell in 10 women. No randomized control. Many were also on standard drugs.

## Tumor

**BENEIN.** Gong, Cho et al., *Advanced Science* 2025;12(3):2402132. DOI [10.1002/advs.202402132](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202402132).

Joint knockdown of MYB, HDAC2, and FOXA2 pushed colon cancer cells toward normal enterocytes in dishes and xenograft mice. Triple knockdown is not a pill.

**HIF protein switch.** Ostermeier and Eshleman, Johns Hopkins, about 2011–2014. A fusion protein turns 5-fluorocytosine into 5-fluorouracil when HIF-1α is present. Dish work. A 2026 post dated it wrong. Delivery was never solved. Do not confuse it with DRP-104.

**DRP-104 (sirpiglenastat).** A masked glutamine blocker (DON) that activates in tumors and inactivates in gut. Early solid-tumor trials (Dracen / Johns Hopkins). A different program from the protein switch.

## Detection

**Galleri (GRAIL).** Multi-cancer blood test. Specificity is high. Sensitivity is about one-third of cancers. A negative test is not a clean bill of health.

**Full-body imaging.** MRI, low-dose CT, and emerging ultrasound. Useful only if a positive finding gets a fast workup. Incidental findings can harm if there is no path to a real test.

## Systemic signal, not a plan you can fill

**Psilocybin.** Kato et al., *npj Aging* 2025. DOI [10.1038/s41514-025-00244-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-025-00244-x). PMID 40628762.

Psilocin delayed senescence in human cells (about 29% more doublings at 10 µM, about 57% at 100 µM) and preserved telomeres. In female mice started at 19 months, monthly oral treatment left 24 of 30 alive versus 14 of 28 on vehicle after 10 months. One sex, late start, survival to a cutoff rather than a finished lifespan curve. No human aging trial. Schedule I in the United States. The published mouse protocol is not a dose for a person.

**Epitalon.** Marketed as a telomerase peptide. Human evidence is weak. Kept on the list so it is not confused with the real shelf. Not a medicine.

## Method, not a dose

**AI-Newton.** Fang et al., arXiv[:2504.01538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01538). From noisy mechanics experiments it rediscovered Newton’s second law, energy conservation, and gravitation, as interpretable concepts that held outside the experiment they were fit on. Physics only. The use here is the question: what concept is common across the regenerative experiments, and what would falsify it in the next tissue?

## Closed

**Photon memory eraser.** Checked against the Night Sky Today post. No paper, no device, no trial. The pictures were a film still and a stock brain. Japanese “photon memory” papers from that period are about computer chips.

The real adjacent work is Goto and Hayashi, *Science* 2021, DOI [10.1126/science.abj9195](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abj9195). Light after learning, plus a delivered photosensitizer, stopped a new mouse memory from consolidating. Mice, a fiber in the brain, gene delivery. The opposite job from dementia. Not a lead for this arsenal.
