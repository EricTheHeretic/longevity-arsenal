# Optogenetics, 2026 Nobel in Physiology or Medicine

Saved 2026-10-06. Method note. Not a drug.

## What was awarded

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel for optogenetics: light-sensitive channelrhodopsins used to switch defined neurons on or off.

The enabling work put algal light-gated ion channels into neurons (reported 2005) so a pulse of light causes a causal, cell-type-specific change in activity.

## What it is for this arsenal

A method, not a medicine. It is how circuits for memory, fear, mood, and movement get mapped. Human use so far is mostly the eye (making remaining retinal cells light-sensitive, as in MCO-010), not editing biography.

The Men in Black memory-eraser rumor stays rejected. See notes/rumor-photon-memory-erasure.md. The real Kyoto light-erasure work (Goto, Hayashi, Science 2021) is mouse LTP erasure inside a short window after learning, and it requires a delivered photosensitizer.

## Why it is in the arsenal

Circuit control is the targeting layer for later brain repair: which cells, which window, then off. Pairs with ER-100, Hevin, and the MDD neurogenesis map. Does not by itself extend lifespan.
