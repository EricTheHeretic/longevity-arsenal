# Jonathan the tortoise — extreme longevity, one animal

Saved 2026-10-14. Research note. Not a medicine.

## Source

- Vaisvil B, Schmitt DP, et al. *Epigenetic insights into extreme longevity in the world’s oldest terrestrial animal, Jonathan.* Science Advances. 7 Oct 2026.
- DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.adw8887.

## What it found

Jonathan is an Aldabra giant tortoise estimated at 194 years. His genome and methylome were compared with other giant tortoises.

He carries 287 unique gene variants in aging pathways, including DNA repair, inflammation, insulin regulation, and tumor suppression. The methylation switches on DNA-repair and metabolism genes, many of them mitochondrial, still resemble those of much younger tortoises.

## Limit

One animal. A candidate-gene map. It does not show that copying any of these variants would extend a human life, and it names no drug.

## Why it is in the arsenal

A natural experiment in which DNA-repair and mitochondrial epigenetic order held for nearly two centuries. Useful as a list of pathways to test, not as a treatment.
