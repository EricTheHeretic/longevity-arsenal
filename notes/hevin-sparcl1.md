# Astrocytic Hevin / SPARCL-1 and cognitive decline

Saved 2026-10-06. Research note. Not a medicine.

## Source

- Cabral-Miranda F, et al. *Astrocytic Hevin/SPARCL-1 Regulates Cognitive Decline in Pathological and Normal Brain Aging.* Aging Cell. 2025 May;24(5):e14493. Epub 2025 Feb 12.
- DOI: 10.1111/acel.14493. PMID: 39935382. PMC: PMC12074016.
- Groups: Federal University of Rio de Janeiro and University of São Paulo. Senior author Flávia Alcantara Gomes.

## What it found

Hevin (SPARCL-1) is a glycoprotein secreted by astrocytes that supports synapses.

AAV driven by a GFAP promoter overexpressed Hevin in hippocampal astrocytes of middle-aged APP/PSEN mice and middle-aged wild-type mice.

- Six months of overexpression improved object-memory discrimination in the Alzheimer-model mice.
- Synaptic marker colocalization rose. Proteomics showed synaptic mediators changed.
- Amyloid plaque load in the hippocampus did not fall. The benefit was synaptic quality, not plaque clearance.
- Wild-type middle-aged mice also improved. A shorter one-month pulse helped early aging.

## Limit

Mouse AAV into the hippocampus. No human trial. Not a pill. Does not clear amyloid.

## Why it is in the arsenal

A repair-the-synapse candidate that works in normal aging and in an Alzheimer model without needing to dissolve plaques. Pairs with ER-100 (eye clock) and the YouthBio brain path as a plasticity tool, not a finished drug.
