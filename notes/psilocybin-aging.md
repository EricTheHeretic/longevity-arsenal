# Psilocybin — cellular lifespan and aged-mouse survival

Saved 2026-10-06. From https://x.com/SciNatureNews/status/2107638790030229906. The post has no paper. The matching study is older than the tweet.

## Paper

Kosuke Kato et al. Psilocybin treatment extends cellular lifespan and improves survival of aged mice. npj Aging. 2025 Jul 8;11(1):55. DOI: 10.1038/s41514-025-00244-x. PMID 40628762. Emory / Baylor.

## What it showed

Psilocin, the metabolite, delayed replicative senescence in human cells. About 29% more population doublings at 10 µM, about 57% at 100 µM, with longer telomeres and less oxidative and DNA-damage signal.

Aged female C57BL/6J mice, started at 19 months. Oral gavage once a month for 10 months: 5 mg/kg the first month, then 15 mg/kg. At the end, 24 of 30 treated mice were alive versus 14 of 28 on vehicle (80% vs 50%, log-rank p = 0.014). Weight loss was similar. Fur looked better; that was not a scored endpoint.

## Limit

One sex. Late start. Survival to a cutoff, not a completed lifespan curve. No human aging trial. Psilocybin is Schedule I in the United States. Psychiatric trials exist; this mouse protocol is not a dose for a person.
