# Urolithin A and stiff-heart failure

Added 2026-10-06 from @scitechgirl. Preclinical.

## Paper

- Su J et al. *Targeting PKGIalpha Cys42 attenuates cardiac dysfunction in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction.* Science Advances. 2026;12(34).
- DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.aec8088
- King’s College London, senior author Joseph Burgoyne. British Heart Foundation funded.

## What they found

Urolithin A, a gut metabolite of ellagitannins in pomegranate, walnut, and berries, activated PKGIalpha at Cys42. In a diet-and-hormone mouse model of HFpEF, one week of treatment improved diastolic function (King’s said up to 80% on the measure they tracked), cut fibrosis and cardiomyocyte enlargement, and increased running. The benefit was gone in mice lacking a functional Cys42. Engineered human heart tissue from stem cells relaxed faster. No abnormal rhythm in that dish model.

## Limit

Mice and lab-grown tissue. Not a human heart-failure trial. Urolithin A supplements already exist for other claims; this paper is not a reason to treat HFpEF with them. Mitopure / Timeline human data are muscle and mitochondrial, not this endpoint.

## Why it is in the arsenal

HFpEF is a large late-life death path with few disease-modifying drugs. A small molecule that helps the heart relax is the right class of tool. Needs a human diastolic-function trial.
