# Urolithin A — heart failure with preserved ejection fraction

Saved 2026-10-06. From a @scitechgirl scan. Research note. Not a prescription.

## Paper

- Su J et al. Targeting PKGIalpha Cys42 attenuates cardiac dysfunction in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction. Science Advances. 2026. 12(34). DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.aec8088
- King's College London, senior author Joseph Burgoyne. King's release 20 Aug 2026.

## What they found

Urolithin A is made by gut bacteria from ellagitannins in pomegranate, walnut, and some berries. In a mouse model of HFpEF it improved diastolic relaxation by up to 80%, cut fibrosis and cardiomyocyte enlargement, and raised running activity after about a week.

Mechanism: it modifies Cys42 on PKGIalpha. Mice lacking that site lost the benefit. Human stem-cell heart tissue relaxed and contracted faster without higher peak force or arrhythmia in the lab report.

## Limit

Mice and dish tissue. Not a heart-failure trial. Gut production of urolithin A varies by microbiome, so food is not a dose. Timeline/Mitopure already sells urolithin A for muscle; that is a different claim.

## Why it is in the kit

HFpEF is about half of heart failure and has few disease-modifying drugs. Heart failure is a top cause of death. This is a small-molecule repair path, not a device.
