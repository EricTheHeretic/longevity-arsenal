# LRP1 polymersomes and amyloid clearance

Added 2026-10-06 from @scitechgirl. Mouse study. Tweet overstated the speed.

## Paper

- *Rapid amyloid-beta clearance and cognitive recovery through multivalent modulation of blood-brain barrier transport.* Signal Transduction and Targeted Therapy. 2025 Oct 7;10:331.
- DOI: 10.1038/s41392-025-02426-1. PMID: 41052971.

## What they found

Polymersomes aimed at LRP1 on the blood-brain barrier biased the receptor toward transcytosis. In Alzheimer-model mice, brain amyloid fell about 45% and plasma amyloid rose about 8-fold within 2 hours by ELISA. The tweet’s “50–60% within one hour” is higher and faster than the paper. After three doses, maze performance looked like wild-type and the authors say the benefit held for months. LRP1–vessel colocalization recovered.

## Limit

Mice. Amyloid clearance has not reliably become a cognition drug in people (aducanumab, lecanemab caveats). No human trial named here.

## Why it is in the arsenal

A clearance mechanism that uses the barrier the brain already has, instead of an antibody that only binds plaque. Worth watching if a human safety study starts. Not a medicine yet.
