# Checked rumor: Japanese photon device that erases human memories

Saved 2026-10-02. Source post: https://x.com/NightSkyToday/status/2106229000653668474

## Verdict

Rumor. Not a human device. The images are a Men in Black neuralyzer still and a stock brain illustration. No paper, no trial, no device name.

Japanese 2026 "photon memory" papers from RIKEN and QST rewrite magnetic computer memory with laser pulses. That is a chip, not a brain.

## The real adjacent science

Kyoto University, Akihiro Goto and Yasunori Hayashi, Science 11 Nov 2021. DOI 10.1126/science.abj9195. "Stepwise synaptic plasticity events drive early phase of memory consolidation."

They expressed a cofilin-SuperNova fusion in mouse hippocampus via AAV. Light releases reactive oxygen and inactivates cofilin, which blocks long-term potentiation. Light right after learning, and again during sleep, stopped a newly formed memory from consolidating. Goto explicitly compared it to the Men in Black flash. Mice only. Fiber in the brain. Gene delivery required.

Follow-on grant KAKENHI-PROJECT-24K22001 (Kyoto, 2024-2026) is an exploratory attempt at a less invasive light method for PTSD, still preclinical.

Engram optogenetics (Tonegawa / Ramirez class) can also silence a specific fear memory in rodents. Same limit: tagged cells, light or a drug, not a flashlight on a person.

## If the real version ever helps neurology

Useful target is a pathological memory, not a person-wipe: PTSD fear trace, some addiction cues. The window is consolidation, minutes to hours, not a lifetime of memories.

It does not treat Alzheimer's. Dementia is loss of memory. Erasure is the opposite job.

Path to a medicine: circuit-specific delivery, a switch, an animal model of one disease memory, then a human safety study with consent and a narrow endpoint (nightmare frequency, cue reactivity). Not a device that erases "the human brain."
