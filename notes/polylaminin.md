# Polylaminin for acute spinal cord injury

Saved 2026-10-14. Research note. Not a medicine.

## Source

- Lima MAB, Menezes K, Coelho-Sampaio T, et al. *Intramedullary injection of polymerized laminin in acute traumatic spinal cord injury: a first-in-human pilot study.* Spinal Cord. 2026.
- DOI: 10.1038/s41393-026-01238-6.
- Tatiana Coelho-Sampaio, Federal University of Rio de Janeiro.

## What it found

Polylaminin is a stabilized polymer of laminin. In rats, an injection into the cord soon after injury supported axons crossing the lesion and some functional recovery.

The human pilot enrolled 8 people with acute complete traumatic injury (AIS A), mean 2.3 days after injury, one intramedullary dose. No serious event was attributed to the injection. Six participants improved by at least two AIS grades, including one who later died after reaching AIS C. Three deaths were reviewed and judged unrelated. Motor or sensory evoked potentials improved in three patients.

## Limit

Open-label, no control, eight people, started in 2016. The authors say the design cannot prove efficacy. As of October 2026 it is not approved, and claims that it will be distributed free are false. A later authorized study is still early.

## Why it is in the arsenal

A scaffold that asks cut axons to cross, instead of only protecting what is left. Worth a controlled trial. Not a cure.
