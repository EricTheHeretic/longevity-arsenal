# Stalled adult hippocampal neurogenesis in major depression

Saved 2026-10-06. Research note. Not a treatment.

## Source

- Peng MS, Dupont MB, et al. *Dysregulated adult hippocampal neurogenesis in major depressive disorder.* Nature Medicine. Published 21 Aug 2026.
- DOI: 10.1038/s41591-026-04571-8. PMID: 42629468.
- Columbia University / New York State Psychiatric Institute. Human postmortem hippocampus.

## What it found

A neurogenic lineage is still present in the adult human hippocampal subgranular zone.

In nonmedicated people with major depressive disorder, that process looks stalled: impaired proliferation and maturation, stress-related reprogramming, interferon signaling, excitatory-inhibitory imbalance, lower metabolic capacity, and weaker synaptic plasticity programs.

This is evidence that hippocampus-dependent symptoms of depression include a plasticity failure, not only a serotonin shortage.

## Limit

Postmortem map. No drug was tested. It does not prove that forcing new neurons will lift depression in a living person.

## Why it is in the arsenal

Names a target class for later tools: restart the stalled neurogenic trajectory, or restore the synaptic programs around it. Detection and mechanism, not a medicine yet.
