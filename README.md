# Longevity Arsenal

Living toolkit of tip-of-the-spear regenerative and longevity programs tracked for the campaign against death.

Research notes only. Not medical advice. Not a dosing guide. Phase 1 means “did not immediately poison people.”

Last updated: 2026-10-01

## Mission

Track medicines and platforms that attack causes of death: cancer, heart and vessel disease, lung failure, frailty, infection, dementia, and the aging rate itself. Prefer repair over pure slowing of decline. Prefer programs that can eventually be manufactured and covered like infrastructure.

## How to read an entry

- **Status** is the clinical stage as of the note date.
- **Job** is what it tries to do if it works.
- **Limit** is why it is not a finished medicine.

## Bone and skeleton

### Fuc-autoBM-MSCs (glycocalyx-edited autologous MSCs)

- **Paper:** Moraleda JM et al. *Glycocalyx-edited mesenchymal stem/stromal cell therapy in advanced osteoporosis.* Cell. Online 11 Sep 2026. DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.017. PMID: 42727576.
- **Trial:** NCT02566655. University of Murcia, Spain. First-in-human Phase 1.
- **What it is:** Patient’s own bone-marrow mesenchymal stem/stromal cells. Surface sugars edited ex vivo so the cells display sialylated Lewis X (sLeX). That acts as a homing tag for bone-marrow vessels. Single intravenous infusion. Named Fuc-autoBM-MSCs in the paper.
- **Who:** 10 women, ages 51–72, advanced osteoporosis, very high fracture risk.
- **Signal:** Fragility-fracture rate fell from 8 events/year in the 2 years before infusion to 0.5 events/year in the 2 years after (~94% reduction). Bone-tissue area rose in 7 of 10 biopsies at ~120 days. Osteoanabolic turnover markers and volumetric BMD moved in the restorative direction. No serious adverse events reported in the paper.
- **Limit:** n=10, no randomized control, many patients were also on standard osteoporosis drugs, cells were not proven to engraft in human bone in this report. Needs a larger randomized trial before anyone calls it a medicine.
- **Why it is in the kit:** Same doctrine as other living pharmacies — take a repair cell, give it an address, send it to the damaged tissue. Bone is a major frailty and death pathway after 70.

## Lung repair

### CMR316

- Inhaled DPP4 inhibitor aimed at stimulating alveolar type 2 stem cells.
- Phase 1/1b completed (NCT06589219). Safety-focused. No efficacy readout yet.

### LTI-03 (Rein Therapeutics)

- Inhaled caveolin-1 peptide. Dual claim: slow fibrotic signaling and spare alveolar progenitors.
- Phase 2 RENEW (~120 IPF patients). FDA Fast Track Aug 2026.

### Buloxibutid / C21 (Vicore)

- Oral AT2 receptor agonist on alveolar type 2 cells.
- Phase 2b ASPIRE enrolled ~378. Topline aimed mid-2027.

### SMS cells (SMSbiotech)

- Nebulized allogeneic blood-derived stem cells for COPD.
- Phase 1b Australia; U.S. study cleared to proceed.

### REGEND001

- Autologous bronchial basal cells returned by bronchoscope. Furthest “put lung stem cells back” program in China. U.S. path still early.

## Vessels and legs

### AMG0001 / Collategene (HGF plasmid)

- Intramuscular hepatocyte growth factor gene therapy to grow small vessels.
- Phase 2 LEGenD-1: faster ulcer healing in chronic limb-threatening ischemia. Not U.S.-approved.

### Humacyte ATEV (Symvess)

- Lab-grown human vessel. Approved for some trauma repairs. PAD / critical limb program still in trials.

## Clock and cell state

### MPTR (Babraham, Gill et al., eLife 2022)

- Dish proof. Yamanaka factors for 13 days on middle-aged human skin fibroblasts, then withdrawn. Clocks ~30 years younger. Still fibroblasts. Not a person.

### ER-100 (Life Biosciences)

- Partial epigenetic reprogramming (OSK, no MYC), AAV into one eye, chemical switch.
- Phase 1 in glaucoma and NAION. First patient dosed June 2026. Safety first.

### YB002 (YouthBio)

- Brain-targeted partial reprogramming path toward Alzheimer’s. Not yet dosed.

## Liver, heart, muscle

### RTX001 (Resolution Therapeutics)

- Engineered autologous macrophages for decompensated cirrhosis.
- EMERALD Phase 1/2 dosing complete Sep 2026. Interim expected Q4 2026.

### STM-01 / iPSC heart patches and spheroids

- Early human heart-muscle cell implants (BioVAT-HF, Heartseed, related programs). Safety and small function signals only.

### Apitegromab-class myostatin blockade

- Muscle-sparing antibody. SMA decision window 2026. Obesity lean-mass data exist. Frailty use is still a hypothesis.

## Detection

### Galleri (GRAIL)

- Multi-cancer blood test. FDA advisory panel Sep 2026: safe, narrower votes on effectiveness. Sensitivity about one-third of cancers; high specificity. Not a negative-test clean bill of health.

### Full-body imaging

- MRI and low-dose CT platforms, plus emerging ultrasound scanners. Useful only if a positive finding gets a fast workup.

## Complex-structure regeneration (not a medicine yet)

### SP6 / SP8 digit program (PNAS 2026)

- Conserved epidermal factors in axolotl, zebrafish, and mouse digit tips.
- Injury-enhancer AAV driving FGF8 partly rescued mouse digit-tip bone. Not a human limb.

### Limb-progenitor factors

- Prdm16, Zbtb16, Lin28a can push mouse cells toward limb-progenitor state. Human dish work is early. Whole-limb regrowth is not a 2030 product.

## Standing rules

1. Add a program only with a paper, trial ID, or company disclosure.
2. Label repair vs slow-the-decline.
3. Never treat a dish result or a tweet as a clinic result.
4. Access matters. A tool that stays in 400 clinics does not move the average.
5. This repo is a map, not a prescription.
